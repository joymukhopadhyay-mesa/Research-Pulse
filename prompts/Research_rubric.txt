RESEARCH PULSE - SCORING RUBRIC
Version 1.0
Owner: Kavita (rubric owner, reviews monthly)
Used by: Gemini Flash (scoring pass and writing pass) and the app's selection code

==================================================================
0. PURPOSE
==================================================================

Kavita is a senior AI strategy consultant. Each Monday she gets one
brief covering her chosen research domains. Her clients ask three
questions:

  Q1. Which architectures should we bet on?
  Q2. Which use cases have real research backing?
  Q3. Which direction is the field moving?

She cares about what is published and validated, not press coverage.
Her biggest risk is MISSING something important, especially in domains
she checks less carefully. The brief must be readable in under 10
minutes.

This rubric decides which papers make the brief and why.

Processing order:
  Layer 1  Gate       -> drop papers that clearly do not belong
  Layer 2  Score      -> Gemini scores each remaining paper
  Layer 3  Select     -> code picks the papers for the brief
  Layer 4  Write      -> Gemini writes a card for each selected paper

==================================================================
1. LAYER 1 - GATE (pass / fail)
==================================================================

A paper is DROPPED only if it clearly fails one of these:

  G1. ON-DOMAIN: The paper's main contribution belongs to one of
      Kavita's domains. A passing mention of a keyword does not count.
  G2. REAL RESEARCH: Not an erratum, duplicate version, withdrawn
      paper, course note or table of contents.
  G3. NEW: Published in the last 7 days and not in a previous brief.
      (G3 is checked by code, not by Gemini.)

RULE: If unsure whether a paper passes the gate, it PASSES.
When in doubt, keep it.

==================================================================
2. LAYER 2 - SCORING (Gemini Flash)
==================================================================

Score each paper on four dimensions, 0 to 3, using ONLY its title and
abstract. Use the definitions below exactly. Do not reward
enthusiastic wording; reward evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------
A. SHIFT SIGNAL                                       weight x3
   Does this paper change what people know or how they think?
------------------------------------------------------------------
  0 = Incremental tweak (e.g. small gain on a single benchmark)
  1 = Solid improvement, nothing surprising
  2 = One clear shift:
        - introduces a NEW BENCHMARK or dataset, OR
        - reports a SURPRISING EVALUATION RESULT (including negative
          results or findings that contradict common belief), OR
        - REFRAMES how a problem is defined or approached
  3 = A strong shift, or more than one shift at once

------------------------------------------------------------------
B. CLIENT RELEVANCE                                   weight x2
   Does it help answer Q1, Q2 or Q3?
------------------------------------------------------------------
  0 = Answers none of the three questions
  1 = Loosely connected to one question
  2 = Clearly informs one question
  3 = Could change what Kavita recommends to a client

------------------------------------------------------------------
C. EVIDENCE STRENGTH                                  weight x2
   Is the claim validated, or only stated?
------------------------------------------------------------------
  0 = Claims with no evaluation
  1 = One benchmark, few or weak baselines
  2 = Several benchmarks, strong baselines, ablations
  3 = All of the above PLUS released code/data OR real-world
      deployment results

------------------------------------------------------------------
D. PRACTICAL MATURITY                                 weight x1
   Can a client act on this?
------------------------------------------------------------------
  0 = Purely theoretical, years from use
  1 = Promising, early stage
  2 = Usable with significant effort
  3 = Usable now

------------------------------------------------------------------
FINAL SCORE
------------------------------------------------------------------
  raw   = (A x 3) + (B x 2) + (C x 2) + (D x 1)      max 24
  score = round(raw / 24 x 10, 1)                    0.0 to 10.0

  The score is calculated by CODE, not by Gemini. Gemini returns only
  the four dimension scores.

------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORING RULES FOR GEMINI
------------------------------------------------------------------
  R1. PROOF: For every dimension scored 2 or 3, quote a short phrase
      (under 15 words) from the abstract that justifies it. If no
      phrase supports the score, lower it to 1.
  R2. UNCERTAIN: If the abstract is too vague to judge, set
      "uncertain": true. Do not guess high or low.
  R3. NO FAME BONUS: Do not raise scores because of the authors'
      lab, company or reputation.
  R4. NO HYPE BONUS: Words like "revolutionary", "breakthrough" or
      "state-of-the-art" earn nothing without numbers behind them.
  R5. PREPRINT: Assume arXiv papers are not peer-reviewed unless the
      abstract states acceptance at a named venue.
  R6. CLIENT QUESTION: Name which of Q1, Q2, Q3 the paper informs
      (one or more), or "none".

------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORING OUTPUT FORMAT (JSON only, no other text)
------------------------------------------------------------------
{
  "paper_id": "string",
  "gate_pass": true,
  "gate_reason": "string, only if gate_pass is false",
  "domains": ["string"],
  "shift": 0,
  "shift_type": ["new_benchmark" | "surprising_result" | "reframing"],
  "client_relevance": 0,
  "client_questions": ["Q1" | "Q2" | "Q3"],
  "evidence": 0,
  "maturity": 0,
  "proof": {
    "shift": "short quote or empty",
    "client_relevance": "short quote or empty",
    "evidence": "short quote or empty",
    "maturity": "short quote or empty"
  },
  "uncertain": false,
  "one_line_reason": "why this paper matters, max 20 words"
}

==================================================================
3. LAYER 3 - SELECTION (app code)
==================================================================

  S1. BAR: Papers scoring 7.0 or higher qualify.

  S2. DOMAIN FLOOR: Every domain gets at least 1 slot if any paper in
      it scores 5.0 or higher. This stops quiet domains from being
      ignored.

  S3. QUIET WEEK NOTICE: If no paper in a domain scores 5.0 or higher,
      print instead:
        "[Domain]: [N] papers scanned, nothing above the bar this week."

  S4. CAPS: Maximum 3 papers per domain. Maximum 12 papers in total.

  S5. MUST-SEE OVERRIDE: A paper with shift = 3 AND evidence >= 2 is
      included even if its domain is already at the cap. It still
      counts toward the total of 12.

  S6. TIE-BREAK ORDER: higher score -> higher shift -> higher evidence
      -> earlier publication date.

  S7. RUNNER-UPS: The next 2 highest papers per domain, plus every
      paper marked "uncertain" with score >= 5.0, go to the appendix
      as title + link only.

  S8. NO DUPLICATES: A paper matching several domains appears once,
      tagged with all its domains, under the domain where it scored
      the highest client relevance.

  S9. COVERAGE LOG: For every domain, record the number of papers
      scanned, gated out, scored and selected. Show the totals in the
      brief's appendix.

==================================================================
4. LAYER 4 - WRITING THE CARDS (Gemini Flash)
==================================================================

Write one card per selected paper. Maximum 120 words per card.
Plain, direct language. No hype.

  WHAT'S NEW      One sentence on the core contribution.
  WHY IT MATTERS  Which shift it shows and why it is significant.
  CLIENT ANGLE    Which client question (Q1/Q2/Q3) it informs and how.
  EVIDENCE        How strong the evidence is. Say "Preprint, not yet
                  peer-reviewed" where it applies.
  VERDICT         Exactly one of:
                    READ IN FULL      - likely to change her view
                    FLAG FOR A CLIENT - directly useful in a client
                                        conversation
                    KNOW IT EXISTS    - worth being aware of
  LINK            The paper's original URL, taken from the source
                  data. Never write or invent a link.

==================================================================
5. IN THE NEWS SECTION (Gemini Flash with Google Search grounding)
==================================================================

  N1. News is NOT scored with this rubric and never raises a paper's
      score.
  N2. 3 to 5 items, maximum 40 words each, across all domains.
  N3. Use only URLs returned by the search grounding. Never write a
      link from memory.
  N4. If a news item is about a paper already in the brief, link the
      two with a note: "Covered above in [Domain]."

==================================================================
6. READ-TIME BUDGET
==================================================================

  Reading speed assumed: 200 words per minute
  Hard limit: 2,000 words (10 minutes)
  Target: 1,600 to 1,800 words (8 to 9 minutes)

    Paper cards     up to 12 x 120 words  = 1,440
    News section    up to 5 x 40 words    =   200
    Headers, quiet-week notes             =   150

  Read time shown at the top of the brief and PDF:
    read_minutes = ceil(word_count / 200)

  If over 2,000 words: drop the lowest-scored non-must-see paper and
  move it to runner-ups. Repeat until under the limit.
  The appendix (links and coverage log) is not counted.

==================================================================
7. FEEDBACK AND TUNING
==================================================================

  F1. Kavita rates each card "useful" or "not useful". Store the
      rating with the paper's four dimension scores.
  F2. Once a month, the rubric owner reviews the ratings. Example: if
      high-maturity papers are often "not useful", lower the maturity
      weight.
  F3. Change one weight at a time. Record each change below.
